Services

What we carry, and how

Two kinds of transport, four kinds of trip, one standard applied to all of them.

Transport type

Wheelchair transport

You stay in your own chair for the entire trip. No transferring into a car seat, no lifting, no being asked to do something your body cannot do today.

Manual and power chairs

Within the rated capacity of the vehicle and its securement system. If a chair is outside that rating, we say so before you book rather than discover it in your driveway.

Four-point securement, every trip

Four straps to the chair, plus a lap and shoulder belt for the passenger. This is not optional and it is not shortened when we are running behind.

Ramp loading, not lifting

Rear-entry ramp access into wheelchair-accessible vehicles. Loading is unhurried, narrated, and done with the passenger, not to them.

Transport type

Ambulatory transport

For passengers who can walk and board with a little help. A cane, a walker, an unsteady morning, or simply nobody available to drive.

A steady arm, offered every time

Assistance to and from the vehicle whether or not it was requested when the trip was booked.

Walkers and mobility aids travel with you

Loaded, secured and returned to you at the door. They do not get left in a trunk at the far end of a parking lot.

Private, never shared

No other passengers, no detours to pick somebody else up, no waiting in a vehicle while a stranger finishes their appointment.

Trip types

Four ways people use us

Standing appointments

Dialysis, infusion, radiation, physical therapy, wound care. Booked once and held on the calendar so nobody has to arrange it again every week.

A note on dialysis. Standing dialysis usually runs Monday, Wednesday, Friday or Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday. Our current operating days cover two of three on either pattern. We will tell you that up front rather than leave you short a chair.

Discharge and transfers

Hospital to home, hospital to skilled nursing, facility to facility. These are the trips that go wrong most often in this industry, usually because nobody answered the phone.

Same-day requests are accepted inside our operating windows when the schedule genuinely allows it. When it does not, you get a clear no quickly, which is worth more than a maybe.

Scheduled one-way and round trip

A single appointment, out and back. We can drop you and return at a set time, or return when you call to say you are finished.

Wait and return

For procedures that require someone to stay on site, or appointments short enough that leaving makes no sense. Your driver stays and brings you home.

Paying for it

How pricing works

Every trip is quoted individually before it is booked. There is no meter running and no invoice that arrives larger than you expected.

A base rate plus loaded mileage

Loaded miles means the miles with you in the vehicle. You are not charged for the drive to reach you.

The number comes first

You get the price in writing before the trip is confirmed, so the decision is yours with full information.

Private pay and facility direct

Families pay us directly. Facilities are invoiced on agreed terms. We do not currently bill Medicaid or transportation brokers.

Straight answers

What we do not do

Saying this plainly is faster for you than finding out at the worst moment.

Stretcher transport

We do not provide stretcher transport. It requires separate licensing, equipment and a second crew member, and we will not accept a trip we are not equipped to run.

Emergency or 911 response

We are not an ambulance service in any form. If it is an emergency, call 911.

Bariatric transport

Requires rated equipment beyond our current capability. We will tell you honestly rather than attempt it.

Trips outside Ohio

Our service is Ohio intrastate. That includes trips into Northern Kentucky, which we do not currently accept.
