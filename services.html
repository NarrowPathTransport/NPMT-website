Services

What we offer

Two types of transport, four types of trips, and the same standard on every one.

Transport type

Wheelchair transport

You stay in your own chair the whole trip. No moving into a car seat, no lifting, and no one asking you to do something your body can’t do today.

Manual and power chairs

As long as the chair fits the rated capacity of our vehicle and tie-down system. If it doesn’t, we’ll tell you before you book, not when we’re in your driveway.

Four-point securement, every trip

Four straps on the chair, plus a lap and shoulder belt for the passenger. We do it every time, even when we’re running behind.

Ramp loading, not lifting

Our wheelchair-accessible vehicles load from a rear ramp. We take our time, talk you through each step, and do it with you, not to you.

Transport type

Ambulatory transport

For passengers who can walk and get in with a little help. Maybe it’s a cane or a walker, a shaky morning, or just nobody available to drive.

A steady arm, offered every time

We help you to and from the vehicle, whether or not you asked for it when you booked.

Walkers and mobility aids travel with you

We load them, secure them, and hand them back to you at the door. They don’t get left in a trunk at the far end of a parking lot.

Private, never shared

No other passengers, no detours to pick someone else up, and no sitting in a vehicle while a stranger finishes their appointment.

Trip types

Four ways people use us

Standing appointments

Dialysis, infusion, radiation, physical therapy, wound care. Book it once and we keep it on the calendar, so nobody has to set it up again every week.

A note on dialysis. Standing dialysis usually runs Monday, Wednesday, Friday or Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, and we run on all of those days. Our hours end at 4:00 PM, so a late chair time may not fit. If it doesn’t, we’ll tell you up front instead of letting you miss a chair time.

Discharge and transfers

Hospital to home, hospital to skilled nursing, or facility to facility. In this industry, these are the trips that go wrong most often, usually because nobody answered the phone.

We take same-day requests during our hours when the schedule truly allows it. When it doesn’t, we’ll tell you no right away, which is more useful than a maybe.

Scheduled one‑way and round trips

A single appointment, there and back. We can drop you off and come back at a set time, or come back when you call to say you’re done.

Wait and return

For procedures where someone has to stay on site, or appointments so short that leaving doesn’t make sense. Your driver waits and brings you home.

Paying for it

How pricing works

Every trip gets its own price before it’s booked. There’s no meter running and no bill that shows up bigger than you expected.

A base rate plus loaded mileage

Loaded miles are the miles you’re actually in the vehicle. You don’t pay for our drive to pick you up.

The number comes first

You get the price in writing before the trip is confirmed, so you can decide with all the facts.

Private pay and facility direct

Families pay us directly. Facilities are invoiced on terms we agree on together. We don’t currently bill Medicaid or transportation brokers.

Straight answers

What we don’t do

Better to hear it now than find out at the worst possible moment.

Stretcher transport

We don’t offer stretcher transport. It takes separate licensing, equipment, and a second crew member, and we won’t take a trip we aren’t equipped to run.

Emergency or 911 response

We’re not an ambulance service of any kind. If it’s an emergency, call 911.

Bariatric transport

It needs rated equipment we don’t have right now. We’ll tell you honestly instead of trying it anyway.

Trips outside Ohio

We only run trips within Ohio. That means we don’t currently take trips into Northern Kentucky.
