About

Why Narrow Path exists.

This company was built by a paramedic who spent years in medical-transport operations and watched the industry fail the people it exists to serve: rides that showed up hours late or never came, dispatch phones no one answered, riders treated like cargo.

One story stays with us. An elderly woman once paid roughly $1,000 out of her own pocket for transportation her insurance already covered, just to avoid the companies that had let her down. She should not have had to. Every rider like her deserves better.

The name is Matthew 7:13-14. The wide path in this industry is easy: hire whoever is available, skip the training, send the van that is good enough, let the phone go to voicemail. The narrow path is harder: show up on time, answer the phone, follow through, and treat every rider as if they were someone you love. When the easy option and the right option diverge, we take the right one.

Equipment and safety

A wheelchair-accessible van with an AMS Vans rear-entry conversion and Q'Straint four-point securement. A written fatigue standard: no one is transported when the driver is impaired by fatigue. After an overnight shift, a trip is moved, reassigned, or declined, never run tired.

Where we are right now

Narrow Path is a newly launched, Ohio-based company. We are completing the authority and registration steps required to operate, and we will not carry a paying passenger until they are in place. We would rather tell you exactly where we stand than overstate it.

Common questions

Straight answers.

How do I schedule a ride?
Get an estimate and request online in under a minute, or call or text (513) 600-6408. We confirm the exact fare and pickup time before anything is booked.
Who can use Narrow Path?
Adults in our service area who use a wheelchair or can walk and board with minimal assistance, going to appointments, dialysis, therapy, hospital discharges, and similar.
What kind of wheelchairs can you take?
Manual and power wheelchairs within our vehicle and securement rating, held with a four-point Q'Straint system. If you are unsure, call and we will talk it through.
Can a family member ride along?
Yes. One family member rides free, every trip.
Do you take insurance or Medicaid?
Not at this time. We are private-pay and facility-pay only, which keeps us direct and accountable to you. We do not bill Medicaid, brokers, or insurance.
What area do you serve?
Five Ohio counties: Hamilton, Butler, Warren, Clermont, and Montgomery. We do not cross into Northern Kentucky. Check your zip on the Service Area page.
What are your hours, and can you do same-day?
Monday through Thursday (closed Friday to Sunday). Same-day trips are accepted within our hours when the schedule allows.
Are you an ambulance?
No. We are non-emergency transportation only. For a medical emergency, call 911.
