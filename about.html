About

Named for a road,
not for a feeling

The wide road in this industry is easy and crowded, and it lets people down every day. We’re choosing the other one on purpose.

The name

Matthew 7:13–14

Wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life.

Matthew 7:13–14

The name isn’t decoration. It describes a choice we make every day, and it’s why we can be clear about what we will and won’t do.

In medical transport, the wide road looks like this. Hire whoever’s available instead of whoever’s right. Skip training because it takes time. Send the vehicle that’s good enough instead of the one that’s clean. Let the phone go to voicemail at the end of a shift. Accept that ninety minutes late is just how it is.

That doesn’t happen because operators are bad people. It happens because the bar in this field is so low that cutting a corner almost never costs anything. Patients can’t easily go somewhere else. Facilities are frustrated but often have no better option. And nobody’s watching closely enough to demand better.

That’s exactly why we hold the line.

Founder

Zachary Russ

Firefighter  /  Paramedic  /  Founder

Russ is a certified firefighter and licensed paramedic. He’s spent his career showing up on the worst day of someone’s life, and he started this company because he kept seeing the same people let down on the ordinary days in between.

Before starting Narrow Path, he was the operations supervisor for a medical transportation operation in the Cincinnati area. He handled scheduling and dispatch, hired and trained drivers, ran vehicle readiness and maintenance, set documentation standards, and wrote the procedures for how a trip actually runs. Every day he talked with nurses, social workers, discharge planners, and schedulers across the region.

He heard the same stories from all of them. Companies showing up hours late. Companies that never came at all. Dispatch lines that rang and rang. Dirty, neglected vehicles. Drivers who never said a word to the person in the back.

He heard from patients too. No air conditioning in July. No heat in January. Being handled like cargo. One patient spent an extra night in the hospital, at their own expense, because the transport company never showed up for the discharge.

Why the fire service matters here. It builds the same habits this work needs. Check your equipment before the shift, not after. Tell people your name. Slow down when someone is scared. Do the boring parts right every single time, because that’s what gets you through the hard parts.

The test we actually use

Would I put my own grandmother in that vehicle, with that driver, on that trip?

If the answer is no, the trip gets moved, the vehicle stays parked, or the person doesn’t get hired. It’s not a slogan on a wall. It’s how we make the call.

It decides who we hire

Character first, then skill. We check driving records before making an offer, run background checks and drug tests, and write our training down instead of assuming people know it.

It decides how we keep our vehicles

A written maintenance program, documented inspections before and after every trip, and a clean interior every morning, not whenever someone gets around to it.

It decides how full our schedule gets

We don’t book more trips than we can run well. A day that’s honestly full beats a day that’s quietly overbooked and falling apart by ten in the morning.

Where the name comes from

A word about faith

Caring for the sick, the elderly, and the vulnerable is at the heart of the Christian faith. For our founder, building this company is a way to serve, not a marketing angle.

You’ll see that in how we operate more than in what we say. It’s why a trip gets moved instead of run by a tired driver. It’s why we’ll tell you we can’t cover a trip instead of taking it and hoping. And it’s why the people in our vehicles are treated like they matter, because they do.

You don’t have to share our faith to ride with us, and no one will ever ask you about it.

What you won’t find here

We hold ourselves to the same honesty

This industry is full of claims nobody checks. Here’s what you won’t see on this website.

No invented history

We won’t claim experience this company hasn’t had, or a track record it hasn’t earned yet. When we have real numbers worth sharing, we’ll share the real ones.

No made-up reviews

There are no testimonials on this site because we don’t have any to share yet. When someone says something worth repeating, we’ll ask their permission first.

No promises we can’t keep

We won’t offer a service we aren’t authorized and equipped to provide. If you ask for something outside what we do, you’ll get a straight no and, when we can, the name of someone who can help.
