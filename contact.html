What to include

Have these details ready before you schedule or call, so we can quickly tell you whether we can help and what the trip will cost.

  • The appointment date and time
  • Pickup address and destination
  • Whether the passenger uses a wheelchair or walks
  • Whether a companion is riding along
  • Whether this is one trip or a standing schedule

Please do not include medical details. The website and ordinary email are not secure channels for health information. Anything clinical belongs in the approved intake process.
