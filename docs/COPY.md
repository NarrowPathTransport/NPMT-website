Write for the passenger, the family member arranging care, and the facility professional. Use plain, direct language. The tone should be calm, practical, warm, and experienced. Do not sound like a hospital system, a marketing agency, or a luxury brand.

## Copy rules

- Lead with the user's next step.
- Prefer concrete language over adjectives.
- Explain non-emergency scope clearly.
- Speak respectfully about passengers and mobility equipment.
- Use `door-through-door` only if that service standard is currently confirmed.
- Use `Request a ride` for every Microsoft Bookings button. Selecting a time is a request: NPT reviews it and confirms the pickup window and price before the trip is final.
- Public phone: (513) 463-7284, or (513) 463-PATH where a memorable version helps. Never publish another number for NPT.
- Keep `quote`, `estimate`, `booking request`, `scheduled`, and `confirmed` distinct.
- Do not write `HIPAA compliant`, `fully insured`, `licensed`, `safe`, `clean record`, `always on time`, or similar claims without current evidence and owner approval.
- Do not use testimonials, volume, outcomes, track record, client names, or facility relationships that Russ has not supplied and approved.
- Do not promise same-day service, a service area, operating hours, a price, or a payment method without current confirmation.

## Privacy language

Use short, visible language near contact and scheduling paths:

> Please do not send diagnoses or other medical details through this website or ordinary email. Use scheduling to begin the conversation, then share only what is needed through the approved intake process.

Do not add fields or copy that invites diagnoses, medical history, insurance information, or identifiable trip details into an unapproved system.

## Service-line language

- Do not advertise stretcher, bariatric, interstate, Kentucky, Ohio Medicaid, or another closed lane.
- Do not turn future plans into `coming soon` language.
- If a service is unavailable, use a plain current-state statement only after Russ confirms it.

## SEO copy rules

- Write unique page titles and descriptions for humans first.
- Use Cincinnati, Southwest Ohio, wheelchair transportation, ambulatory transportation, and non-emergency medical transportation only where accurate and natural.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, duplicated location blocks, fabricated city pages, or location claims beyond the confirmed operating area.
- Keep canonical URLs, Open Graph text, sitemap entries, and visible copy aligned.
